Resources

Most of our published tools are freely available from public repositories. If you can't find what you need, or want something new built, get in touch.

Addgene (plasmids & AAVs)

Our deposited plasmids and AAVs, including GCaMP calcium indicators and the iGluSnFR, iGABASnFR and other neurotransmitter sensors.

Looger Lab on Addgene →

Bloomington (fly lines)

UAS lines for jGCaMP8f, jGCaMP8m and jGCaMP8s (with the GENIE Project) and iGluSnFR. Details of the jGCaMP8 lines are in their FlyBase record.

Search the Bloomington catalog →

JAX (mouse lines)

Thy1-GCaMP6 transgenic mice made by the GENIE Project, such as Thy1-GCaMP6s line GP4.3 (JAX stock 024275).

View at JAX →

ZFIN (zebrafish lines)

Transgenic zebrafish expressing our sensors, such as GCaMP and iGluSnFR, are catalogued in the Zebrafish Information Network.

Search ZFIN →

Collaborations & custom reagents

We are always open to collaboration. We regularly engineer new sensors and other protein tools for other labs, adapt existing ones to new targets, model organisms or imaging setups, help test them in new systems, and design custom AAV capsids for efficient, targeted gene delivery to specific cell types, tissues or species. Companies interested in our technologies are welcome to reach out too.

To discuss a project, email Loren Looger at llooger [at] health.ucsd.edu.
