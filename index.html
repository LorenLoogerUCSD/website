Looger Lab

The Looger Lab uses protein engineering to create tools for studying and manipulating the brain and other biological systems. Combining computational and evolutionary methods, we develop sensors for calcium, neurotransmitters and neuromodulators, optogenetic effectors, engineered viral capsids, and probes for EM and immunolabeling, and we test them together with collaborators. More recently we have also been designing proteins and pathways for carbon capture and environmental remediation.
